Percolation Days Grenoble-Sorbonne

On November 30 2026, at Grenoble

What is it?

We are launching a bi-annual event in percolation theory devoted to hosting self-contained mini-courses on beautiful percolation topics. Our first edition will take place at Grenoble, on November 30, 2026. Mini-courses will start at 13:30 and end at 17:45.

What are we gonna learn?

For the first edition, we will have the pleasure to attend lectures by Paul Drouvillé and Malo Hillairet. Paul will talk about supercritical sharpness of Bernoulli percolation on transitive graphs and Malo will explain to us the infinite incipient cluster.
